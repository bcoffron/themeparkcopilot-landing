Common questions
What happens when my 7-day trial ends?
If you haven’t subscribed, you’ll see a paywall prompt in the app. Your trial data and 2-day schedule are preserved — subscribe at any time to unlock the remaining days instantly.
Why does the trial only generate 2 days?
The 2-day limit lets you fully experience the app — live wait routing, AI Optimize, and contextual scheduling all work on your real trip days. Remaining days generate the moment you subscribe.
Does one plan cover a whole resort?
Yes — one trip covers the whole resort. Disneyland Resort (both parks) counts as one trip. Walt Disney World (all four parks) counts as one trip. Visiting both on the same vacation is two separate trips.
Can my whole family use one subscription?
Yes. Every plan includes both an admin trip code and guest codes for your whole group. Everyone sees the same real-time schedule on their own device — no extra charge per person.
What parks are supported?
Disneyland Resort and Walt Disney World are live at launch. Universal Studios Hollywood, Universal Orlando, and more are coming soon.
